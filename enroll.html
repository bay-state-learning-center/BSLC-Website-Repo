Financial aid is available

BSLC is committed to being an economically diverse community. We offer need-based scholarship assistance on a limited basis. Submit a financial aid application alongside your membership packet. Applying doesn't guarantee aid, but we want cost to be as low a barrier as possible — please reach out if you have questions.

Semester commitment

Members who start at the beginning of a semester make a five-month commitment when registering. Semester payments are due August 15 and January 15. Monthly installments are due by the 15th of each month.

Payment options

Pay in full each semester, or break it into monthly installments via credit card, debit card, or ACH auto-pay. Credit card payments may incur processing fees. No refunds are issued for early termination within a billing cycle.

Joining late

New members joining mid-semester pay the first month's tuition (or the remaining semester balance) at the time they submit their registration materials, and commit through the end of that semester.

Questions?

Reach our operations director at information@baystatelearning.org or 781-355-6575.

Part-time membership

We offer part-time arrangements for families whose situations call for a more flexible schedule. Part-time is an option, though we generally recommend full-time membership for more effective learning and community-building.

If you'd like to discuss part-time logistics and fees, get in touch at information@baystatelearning.org or 781-355-6575.
