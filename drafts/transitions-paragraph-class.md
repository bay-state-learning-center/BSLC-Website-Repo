Hello Bay State family, friends, and passers-by! My name is Brian, and this year I’ll be taking on some of the behind-the-scenes work at BSLC, including this very blog! To set expectations, I’m not planning on writing in-depth weekly class analyses, but rather occasional check-ins with a few photos from a class or two and what the kids got up to in those classes. So without further ado, let’s check in on last week’s Transitions class!

Transitions is a class where students focus on life beyond their time at Bay State, whether that’s college, career, or independent life skills. It’s usually led by Terry, but last week JayJay stepped in as a guest teacher, focusing on the humble paragraph and how it can connect those things and build toward larger forms like essays and creative writing. Students wrote paragraphs on topics ranging from their favorite shows to imagining life as a woodland creature.

Check back soon for another peek into a class at BSLC, and please don’t hesitate to reach out to [info@baystatelearning.org](mailto:info@baystatelearning.org) with any questions or anything specific you’d like to see!

–Brian
