2026–27 Classes

Our class lineup changes every year based on the needs and interests of our members, and is updated in the fall. This list reflects the 2026–27 year. Browse previous years' classes.

Math & Science

  • The Math Lab · Amelia Real math is about spotting patterns, visualizing relationships, and solving problems creatively. Each week, members tackle a new puzzle, game, art project, or thought experiment — from infinitely typing monkeys to trickster game-show hosts to the mathematics of flowers.
  • Statistics · Amelia The mathematical study of data, with applications in science, economics, and everyday life. Members explore data visualization, probability, and statistical inference, working toward a student-directed research project.
  • Math Practice · Amelia One-on-one or small-group math tutoring — sign up for as little as ten minutes or as much as an hour. Members build math fluency at their own pace, using whichever resources work best for them.

Language & Literature

  • Books on Brains · Amelia A non-fiction, read-aloud book club diving into behavior, brains, and social science.
  • Books and Tea Time · Terry A collaborative reading group where members choose the books and lead the discussion each week. Outside reading is required.

Humanities

  • Critical Thinking · JayJay & Amelia Members practice analyzing contemporary media through the lens of rhetorical appeals, logical fallacies, statistical literacy, and how algorithm-driven and AI-generated content shapes our thinking.
  • Current Events · Terry News through the lens of media criticism — evaluating sources, spotting bias, and distinguishing reporting from propaganda and misinformation. Members bring their own stories to discuss.

Arts & Making

  • Photography & Image Editing · Terry Technique, lighting, and the art of making images — from smartphone to digital SLR — plus a look at the history of photography along the way. Members build a portfolio of their best work through image-editing and multimedia projects.
  • Handwork Hour · Terry A collaborative making session where everyone brings their skills to share. It's not about what you make — it's about enjoying the process of working with your hands alongside others.
  • Watercolor Class · Keith (volunteer) An introduction to watercolor technique — brushes, paper, and color theory — with subjects ranging from landscapes and still lifes to portraits. Members build basic drawing skills, explore mixed media, and learn to give and receive kind, constructive feedback in a low-pressure environment.
  • Beginner Drawing · Lee (volunteer) An introduction to drawing for beginners, starting with simplifying subjects into shapes and building toward perspective and lighting.
  • Artists' Workshop · Lee (volunteer) A critique group for 2D artists looking to improve their work. Each week, one or two members share a piece for group discussion, with each artist setting their own comfort level for feedback.
  • Windworld RPG · Lee (volunteer) A tabletop role-playing game set in a fantastical, ever-changing world designed by Lee. A great fit for Dungeons & Dragons fans, or anyone curious about RPGs without the intimidating rulebook.

Life & Transitions

  • Life Skills and Financial Literacy · Rebekah (volunteer) and Terry Members build practical life skills, with a strong focus on financial literacy. Topics include budgeting, résumés and interviews, professionalism, physical and mental health, drug and alcohol safety, and using your own learning style to your advantage.
  • Nature · Terry The whole point is to get outside — nature is the teacher. Hiking, fishing, growing plants hydroponically and outdoors, documentaries, cooking projects, indigenous ecological knowledge, and whatever else the class dreams up.
  • Inquiry Projects · Terry Each member picks a big idea or issue and dives deep, researching their topic like a journalist. Projects wrap up with a final presentation — anything from visual displays and art projects to infographics and hands-on builds.
  • Transitions Program · Terry For members in their final year (or two) at BSLC, blending academic readiness with real-world experience. Includes HiSET prep, college and community-college guidance, weekly check-ins, and internship and mentorship opportunities.
