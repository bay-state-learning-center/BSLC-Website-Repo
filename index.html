Bay State Learning Center

Individualized learning with
no grades and no tests.

What we do

Bay State Learning Center is an engaging, compassionate alternative to traditional school. We offer small classes, creative activities, one-on-one mentoring, and preparation for college and beyond.

We welcome teens from all educational backgrounds who are ready for something different. At BSLC, your interests shape your classes, projects, and mentoring, all with the support of a learning community that's rooting for you.

Who we are

For over a decade, we have intentionally cultivated a community that celebrates individual differences. When teens feel welcome and valued—exactly as they are, right now—they develop the confidence to pursue their interests and explore new opportunities.
